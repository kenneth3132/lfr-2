# Line Follower Robot (LFR) — Bill of Materials & Hardware Specifications

## 1. Executive Summary
This document provides the complete hardware specification, Bill of Materials (BOM), electromechanical design rules, and electrical connection architecture for the high-performance Arduino Nano PID Line Follower Robot (LFR).

The robot utilizes a differential drive configuration with high-speed N20 micro metal gearmotors, driven by a Toshiba TB6612FNG dual MOSFET H-bridge motor driver. Line sensing is achieved using an 8-channel TCRT5000 / RSL 08 reflectance sensor array directly wired to the analog inputs (A0–A7) of an ATmega328P microcontroller running at 16 MHz.

## 3. Subsystem Specifications & Engineering Guidelines

### 3.1. Microcontroller: Arduino Nano (ATmega328P)
- **Clock**: 16 MHz external crystal (16 MIPS).
- **Supply Logic**: 5.0V DC regulated.
- **Memory**: 32KB Flash, 2KB SRAM, 1KB EEPROM.
- **EEPROM Storage**: Non-volatile calibration values (`whiteMin[8]`, `blackMax[8]`, `centerPos`) are preserved across power cycles using a magic byte (`0x5A`).
- **ADC Configuration**: 8 direct analog channels (A0 to A7). Eliminates external multiplexers (CD4051), allowing sequential conversions with minimum latency.
- **PWM Channels**: Timer 0 hardware PWM on pins D5 (PWMB) and D6 (PWMA) running at ~976 Hz.

### 3.2. Motor Driver: Toshiba TB6612FNG Dual H-Bridge
- **Technology**: Power MOSFET H-bridges with low ON-resistance ($R_{DS(on)} \approx 0.5\,\Omega$).
- **Efficiency Advantage over L298N**: The bipolar L298N drops 1.8V - 2.5V as heat. The TB6612FNG drops less than 0.2V at nominal load, delivering higher voltage to the motors with virtually zero heat generation.
- **Ratings**:
  - Motor Supply Voltage ($V_M$): 2.5V to 13.5V (operating at 7.4V - 11.1V).
  - Logic Supply Voltage ($V_{CC}$): 2.7V to 5.5V (connected to 5.0V regulated bus).
  - Continuous Current: 1.2A per channel.
  - Peak Current: 3.2A per channel.
- **STBY Pin Requirement**:
  - The TB6612FNG `STBY` (Standby) pin **MUST BE TIED DIRECTLY TO THE 5V LOGIC BUS**. If `STBY` is floating or grounded, the motor driver outputs remain in high-impedance (tri-state) shutdown and the motors will not spin.

### 3.3. Sensor Array: RSL 08 / TCRT5000 8-Channel Reflectance Array
- **Optics**: 8x TCRT5000 infrared emitter-phototransistor pairs spaced along a straight bar.
- **Output**: 8 analog voltage lines connected to Arduino pins A0–A7.
- **Mounting Guidelines**:
  - **Ground Clearance Requirement: MUST maintain 3mm to 5mm ground clearance.**
  - Distance < 2.5mm: Optical clipping and blind spot occurs because emitter beam and phototransistor focal cones do not overlap.
  - Distance > 6.0mm: Infrared return intensity drops off rapidly ($1/r^2$), degrading signal contrast between black and white.

### 3.4. Actuators: N20 Micro Metal Gearmotors
- **Operating Voltage**: 6.0V Nominal (capable of 7.4V with PWM limit).
- **Gear Ratio & Speed**: 10:1 to 30:1 gear ratio providing 600–1000 RPM at 6V.
- **Stall Current**: ~0.8A to 1.2A per motor at stall.
- **Noise Suppression**: Solder a 100nF (0.1uF) ceramic capacitor across each motor's solder lugs to suppress brush commutation noise.

### 3.5. Power Distribution & Star Grounding Topology
- **Battery**: 2S (7.4V) or 3S (11.1V) LiPo battery, 450mAh - 850mAh, 25C+.
- **Step-Down Regulator**: Mini-360 or LM2596 DC-DC buck converter stepping battery voltage down to 5.0V DC (2A-3A output).
  - Do **not** power the system via the Arduino Nano's onboard linear regulator (AMS1117). The 8 sensor LEDs alone draw ~160mA; at 11.1V input, the linear regulator would dissipate over 1.2W and shut down thermally.
- **Bulk Decoupling**: A 470uF to 1000uF 25V low-ESR electrolytic capacitor placed directly across TB6612FNG `VM` and `GND` absorbs inductive transient spikes and prevents brownouts during sudden motor reversals.
- **Star Grounding**: High-current motor ground returns must **never** be daisy-chained with sensitive analog sensor grounds. Connect Battery (-), TB6612 GND, Buck GND, Nano GND, and Sensor GND to a single central Star Ground junction point.

## 5. Pre-Flight Hardware Checklist
- [ ] **Sensor Height**: Verify 3mm to 5mm ground clearance between TCRT5000 lenses and track.
- [ ] **TB6612 STBY**: Confirm `STBY` pin is hardwired to 5V.
- [ ] **Pushbuttons**: Confirm Button 1 is between D11 & GND, Button 2 is between D12 & GND (no connection to 5V).
- [ ] **Star Ground**: Verify 0-ohm continuity between battery negative, driver ground, buck ground, sensor ground, and Arduino ground.
- [ ] **Decoupling Capacitor**: 470uF - 1000uF capacitor installed across `VM` and `GND` with correct polarity.
- [ ] **Buck Voltage**: Pre-adjust buck converter to exactly 5.0V DC before connecting to the Arduino Nano 5V pin.
